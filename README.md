# The Permanent Establishment Hub

> A comprehensive study companion and working calculator for international tax practitioners and CA / EA / CPA students. Covers all five types of Permanent Establishment (PE) across 16 major tax treaties, with 25 landmark case law rulings, six interactive calculators, a step-by-step PE determination wizard, an Article 7 profit attribution worksheet, and a 40-card flashcard study mode.

**Live tool:** Open `index.html` in any browser, or deploy via GitHub Pages / Netlify (instructions below).

---

## What's inside

### 1. PE Map
Visual overview of all five PE types — Fixed Place (Art. 5(1)–(4)), Construction (Art. 5(3)), Service (Art. 5(2)(l)), Agency (Art. 5(5)–(6)), and India's Significant Economic Presence (Sec. 9(1)(i) Expl 2A). Each card explains the threshold and one-click navigates to the relevant calculator.

### 2. PE Wizard
A guided decision tree that walks you through the determination logic step by step. Starts from "Is there any presence?" and branches through fixed-place, construction, service, agency, and subsidiary scenarios — landing on a definitive verdict with the supporting article and case law.

### 3. Six Calculators
- **Fixed Place PE** — Six-condition test (existence, fixity, permanence, disposal, business activity, non-preparatory)
- **Construction PE** — Multi-treaty (16 treaties) duration test with MLI Article 14 contract-splitting check
- **Service PE** — Multi-treaty, related/unrelated party thresholds, physical-presence requirement per Clifford Chance (Dec 2025)
- **Agency PE** — Tests pre-BEPS habitual conclusion + post-BEPS principal role + MLI Article 12 commissionnaire
- **Significant Economic Presence (India)** — Sec. 9(1)(i) Expl 2A revenue and user thresholds
- **Anti-Fragmentation** — BEPS Action 7 / MLI Article 13 four-factor test

### 4. Treaty Atlas
Side-by-side comparison of PE thresholds across 16 major treaties: OECD Model 2017, UN Model 2017, US Model 2016, India-US, India-UK, India-Singapore, India-Mauritius, India-Netherlands, India-China, India-Germany, India-Japan, India-UAE, India-Hong Kong, India-Australia, India-Canada, US-Canada.

### 5. Profit Attribution Worksheet
Article 7 OECD Authorised Approach computation with India-specific caveats (Sec. 44C 5% cap on head office expenses, restrictions on notional payments, limited force-of-attraction add-back).

### 6. Case Law Library
Twenty-five landmark rulings with citations and holdings — Formula One (SC 2017), E-Funds (SC 2017), Morgan Stanley (SC 2007), Samsung Heavy Industries (SC 2020), Clifford Chance (Delhi HC Dec 2025), Galileo, Rolls Royce, UAE Exchange, Centrica India, GE Energy Parts, and more.

### 7. Glossary
Thirty PE-related terms with citations — covers every concept that appears in a determination.

### 8. Study Mode
Forty flashcards organized into eight categories (Definitions, Thresholds, Concepts, Case Law, IT Act, BEPS/MLI, Attribution). Arrow-key navigation, spacebar to flip.

## Deployment

### Option A — GitHub Pages (recommended)

1. Create a new public repository on GitHub (suggested name: `permanent-establishment-hub`)
2. Upload all files in this folder (`index.html`, `README.md`, `LICENSE`) — drag and drop in the browser
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**
4. Under "Build and deployment," select **Deploy from a branch → main → / (root)**
5. Save. Within 2–3 minutes, your hub is live at:
   ```
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/permanent-establishment-hub/
   ```

### Option B — Netlify Drop (faster, no account)

1. Visit `app.netlify.com/drop`
2. Drag the entire folder onto the page
3. You get a live URL in under a minute (free, public)
4. Optionally claim the site by signing up — gives you a custom subdomain

### Option C — Local use

Just open `index.html` in any browser. Everything is self-contained; no server required. Works offline.

## What this is NOT

- **Not legal or tax advice.** This is a study aid built from public sources. PE determinations in real engagements depend on specific facts and require qualified professional review.
- **Not exhaustive.** Sixteen treaties are covered; India has 90+ DTAAs and US has 60+. For unlisted treaties, use the OECD Model as a baseline and consult the actual treaty text.
- **Not real-time.** Treaty thresholds and case citations are current as of the build date. Verify against the latest Finance Acts, MLI notifications, and judicial pronouncements for live engagements.
